What the service does

CleanSheets reads the transactions on a bank account you connect, lets you assign each one to a category, and adds the amount to that category's Actual column in a Google Sheet you own. That is the whole of it.

What it does not do

  • It cannot move money. Bank access is read-only and no payment can be initiated.
  • It is not financial, tax, or investment advice. The figures it shows come from your bank and your own spreadsheet.
  • It does not create or manage your budget for you. The categories and budgets are whatever you wrote in your spreadsheet.

Your spreadsheet is yours, and it is edited

The point of the app is that it writes to your spreadsheet. Each save adds an amount to a cell and records a row in a hidden _log tab. Saves can be reversed from the app, which subtracts the amount again.

Check your sheet. You are responsible for the contents of your own document. Keep a copy if it matters to you — Google Sheets keeps version history, and we recommend relying on it rather than on us.
