CleanSheets

Your bank, in the spreadsheet you already use

CleanSheets is a personal budgeting app for iPhone. It reads the transactions on a bank account you connect, shows them one at a time, and writes the amount into a Google Sheet that you own — the same budget you were already keeping by hand.

The short version. Your bank access is read-only, so nothing can be moved or paid. Your spreadsheet stays yours: the app adds amounts to it and never deletes it. Nothing is sold, shared for advertising, or used to build a profile of you, and there is no analytics or tracking in the app.

How it works

  • Connect your bank. Through a licensed account-information provider, using your bank's own login. Read-only, and you can withdraw it at any time.
  • Point it at your sheet. You pick the spreadsheet, the month's tab, and which sections the app may write into. Nothing else in your Drive is read.
  • File what arrives. Each transaction is one tap into a category. The amount lands in that category's Actual column, and you can undo a save for a few seconds afterwards.

What it does not do

It does not move money, restructure your spreadsheet, or decide your categories for you. The sheet's own layout is the source of truth — the app reads the categories and budgets you already wrote, and adds to them.

Status

CleanSheets is in development and not yet on the App Store. If you have arrived here from a consent screen, this is the app asking for access to the spreadsheet you chose.
