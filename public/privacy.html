CleanSheets

Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 October 2026

CleanSheets is a personal budgeting app. It reads the transactions on a bank account you connect, lets you file each one into a category, and writes the amount into a Google Sheet that you own. This page describes exactly what data that involves, where it goes, and how to get rid of it.

The short version. Your bank access is read-only. Your spreadsheet stays yours and is never deleted by us. Nothing is sold, shared for advertising, or used to build a profile of you. There is no analytics or tracking in the app.

Who is responsible

CleanSheets is operated by its developer as an individual. For any question about your data, including access or deletion, write to k.danilovs2905@gmail.com.

What data is involved

DataWhy
Your Google account identity — email address and account identifier To sign you in and to know which spreadsheet and bank connection are yours.
A list of your Google Drive spreadsheets — names and identifiers only So you can choose which spreadsheet to use. The contents of other files are never read.
The contents of the one spreadsheet you choose To read your categories and budgets, and to add amounts to the Actual column when you file a transaction.
Your bank account and its recent transactions — merchant, amount, date, status To show you what is waiting to be categorised. Access is read-only; no payment can be initiated.

Where it is stored

  • On the server: your account record, your chosen spreadsheet and column settings, an encrypted Google authorisation token, and your bank session reference.
  • On your phone: sign-in tokens in the iOS Keychain, a cache of your transaction queue and categories, and your own app settings such as reminder time. This is a cache — the authoritative copies are your bank and your spreadsheet.

Transaction data is not stored on the server. It is fetched from your bank when the app asks, passed to the app, and not retained.

Who else is involved

Four service providers process data on our behalf, and no one else receives it:

  • Google — sign-in, and reading and writing your chosen spreadsheet.
  • Enable Banking — the regulated connection to your bank under PSD2.
  • Supabase — the database holding the account record described above.
  • Vercel — hosting for the service.

Your data is not sold, rented, or used for advertising, and it is not shared with anyone else.

Your bank connection

The connection is made through Enable Banking under PSD2, using your bank's own login and strong authentication. We never see your bank credentials. The consent is read-only and expires on a schedule your bank sets — typically 90 days — after which you must renew it deliberately. You can revoke it at any time, in the app or with your bank.

Your spreadsheet

The app writes to one spreadsheet you choose: it adds amounts to the Actual column of the categories you pick, and keeps a hidden _log tab recording what it filed, so the same transaction is never counted twice. It never edits your category names, your budgets, or any other file in your Drive.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete account removes your account and everything stored about you on the server, and clears the app's data from your phone. We also attempt to revoke the Google and bank authorisations.

Your spreadsheet is deliberately not deleted, including its hidden _log tab. It is your own document, and it stays in your Google Drive exactly as it is. Deleting it is yours to do, if you want to.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access to your data, correction, erasure, restriction of processing, or a copy in portable form, and you may object to processing. Email the address above and you will get an answer. You also have the right to complain to your national data protection authority — in Denmark, Datatilsynet.
